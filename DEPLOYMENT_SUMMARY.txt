For ALL 27 target schools, automatically:

1. FETCHES current roster from school website
2. IDENTIFIES Olympic swimmers on team
3. FINDS international/foreign athletes
4. LOCATES tall swimmers (6'3"+) matching Michael
5. CALCULATES recruiting fit score (0-100)
6. GENERATES 3-document package per school
7. RE-RANKS schools based on discoveries
8. CREATES GitHub Issue with findings
